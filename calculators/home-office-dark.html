SARS IN28 requirements: Office must be (1) specifically equipped for trade, (2) used regularly and exclusively for trade, (3) employees need written employer confirmation, and (4) where your trade is employment, more than 50% of duties must be performed there.
Your Situation
Salaried employees (s23(m)): you may claim rent, rates & taxes, electricity, cleaning, security and levies (apportioned by floor area) — but bond interest is disallowed from the 2023 year (IN28). You also need written employer confirmation that you are required to work from home, and more than 50% of your duties must be performed in the home office.

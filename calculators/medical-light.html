Medical Aid Membership
Disability scope: applies only to the taxpayer, spouse, or child (own or spouse's child). Extended family members do not qualify for full s6B treatment.
Out-of-Pocket Medical Expenses
Qualifying: registered medical practitioners, dentists, optometrists, hospitals, nursing homes, pharmacist for prescribed medicines. Not qualifying: OTC medication, cosmetic procedures, gym fees, supplements (unless prescribed), amounts recovered from scheme. Out-of-pocket expenses can be claimed even without a medical aid.

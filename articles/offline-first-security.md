## Why Your Tax Data Is Different

Tax information is sensitive. It contains:
- Your income figures
- Deduction amounts
- Personal financial details
- Business structure information

If this data is compromised, you face:
- Identity theft risk
- Targeted fraud
- Unnecessary audit risk
- Privacy violations

Most people don't realize that uploading their tax information to cloud-based calculators and spreadsheet tools puts it at risk, even if those services are reputable.

## The Cloud Problem

Cloud-based tax tools operate on this model:
1. You enter your financial data
2. Data travels to their servers
3. Servers calculate results
4. Results come back to you
5. **Your data is now stored somewhere, potentially indefinitely**

Even if a service deletes your data after you're done, it may be:
- Backed up on their servers
- Cached in multiple geographic locations
- Accessible to support staff
- Subject to data retention laws you're not aware of
- Vulnerable to breaches

Major corporations have suffered data breaches affecting millions. A smaller tax tool is no different—and perhaps less secure than enterprises with dedicated security teams.

## What Is Offline-First?

Offline-first technology works differently:

1. **All processing happens on your device** - Your computer, not someone else's server
2. **No data transmission** - Your financial information never leaves your device
3. **Local encryption** - Data at rest is encrypted with military-grade AES-GCM-256
4. **You retain control** - You decide what to keep and what to delete
5. **No account required** - No usernames, passwords, or account recovery needed

This is fundamentally more secure because the biggest attack surface—network transmission and remote storage—is completely eliminated.

## Understanding AES-GCM-256

InspiredTax uses **AES-GCM-256** encryption for any data you choose to save locally:

**AES** = Advanced Encryption Standard
- US Government standard encryption algorithm
- Used worldwide by military, banks, and intelligence agencies
- Mathematically proven to be secure
- No known practical attacks against proper AES-256 implementation

**GCM** = Galois/Counter Mode
- A mode of operation that provides both confidentiality and authenticity
- Detects if encrypted data has been tampered with
- Ensures data integrity

**256** = Key size in bits
- 256-bit keys have 2^256 possible combinations
- Would take longer than the age of the universe to brute-force
- Standard for highly sensitive government data

**In plain English:** Your data is encrypted with the same strength algorithm that protects classified government information.

- They decide to share anonymized data with "partners"

## When Offline Might Have Limitations

We're honest about this: offline-first isn't perfect for everything.

Offline-first works best when:
- You want fast, private calculations
- You don't need cloud sync across devices
- You're okay storing data locally
- You want maximum security

Offline-first is less ideal if you:
- Need to access data on multiple devices simultaneously
- Require automatic cloud backup
- Want customer support to troubleshoot your specific calculations
- Need to collaborate with an accountant in real-time

For most people doing provisional tax planning alone, offline-first is ideal. If you need collaboration, you can export results and share securely.

## Best Practices for Offline Security

Even with encryption, best practices matter:

1. **Use a strong password** for your device if you save encrypted files
2. **Back up your files** to secure locations (external drive, personal cloud storage)
3. **Never share your files** unless you trust the recipient completely
4. **Update your software** to get security patches
5. **Use a password manager** if you need to remember complex passwords

## The Fortnight That Buys the Whole Year

There is exactly one moment when tax admin costs nothing: the start, before transactions accumulate. A freelancer who spends two hours in early March building the year's scaffolding does trivial maintenance for twelve months and computes February's payment in an evening. One who doesn't spends next January reconstructing a year from bank statements. Same tax bill — wildly different lives.

Here's the setup, in the order that compounds best.

## 1. Load the Year's Parameters

The Budget's numbers are now law-in-effect (or shortly to be gazetted): pull the **2027/28 tax table and rebates** from the published SARS tables and put them at the top of a fresh estimate working paper. Note this year's **s11F cap** and the medical credit amounts. Five minutes — and it inoculates you against the classic error of estimating an entire year on last year's table.

## 2. Recalibrate the Set-Aside

Compute last year's effective rate — total tax over taxable income, both sitting in your February working paper. Add three to five points of margin (the set-aside applies to gross receipts; tax applies to profit), then adjust for Budget reality: if the tables were frozen or under-adjusted, inflationary rate increases will lift your effective rate this year — nudge the percentage up a point. Set the standing instruction on the tax account to the new figure, effective from the first March receipt.

If last February required borrowing or a scramble despite a set-aside, the percentage was wrong — this is the annual moment to fix it, not mid-crisis.

## 3. Reset the Physical Systems

- **Logbook**: opening odometer entry (you photographed it on the 28th — that photo is the entry), fresh page or app-year, and the business-percentage habit continues from trip one.
- **Records folders**: clone last year's structure — income, expenses, vehicle, home office, retirement-medical, SARS correspondence, working papers — empty, dated 2027/28. Sixty seconds now; the alternative is a shoebox in November.
- **Asset register**: roll it forward — this year's opening tax values, last year's additions carried in, anything scrapped or sold flagged for the recoupment reconciliation.
- **Invoice numbering and turnover tab**: new sequence if you use yearly ones, and reset the rolling 12-month VAT-threshold tracker. The R1 million line doesn't care about tax years, but March is when the tracking habit either continues or dies.

## 4. Diarise the Year's Four Dates — Plus Two Soft Ones

Hard deadlines, with reminders a month and a week out:

- **31 August 2027** — first 2027/28 IRP6 and payment.
- **30 September 2027** — voluntary top-up for the 2026/27 year just ended (the cheap fix if February's estimate undershot).
- **Filing season deadline** — your 2026/27 ITR12, per the dates SARS announces mid-year; provisional taxpayers historically run into January 2028.
- **29 February 2028** — second period; a leap year gives you an extra day and it's a Tuesday, but the payment-clearance discipline is identical.

Soft dates that earn their calendar space: **early October** — the RA planning session (ceiling, gap, funding pattern), and **early December** — the pre-February review. Both exist as articles on this site; the calendar entries are just links to your future self.

## 5. Set the Year's One Improvement

Every year-end exposes a weakest link — the receipts that weren't photographed, the logbook gap in July, the estimate working paper that didn't exist. Pick **one** and systematise it now, while the pain is fresh. The monthly 15-minute close (reconcile income, file documents, update the logbook summary) remains the highest-yield habit in the freelance tax toolkit: it's the difference between *having* records and *finding* them.

## 6. A Fresh Look at the Structures

March is also the natural annual checkpoint for the bigger, slower questions — not to churn them, but to notice when an answer has changed:

- **Income trajectory vs the R1.8m paragraph 20 line** — crossing it changes your February obligations from safe-harbour arithmetic to 80%-of-actual precision; better to know in March than discover in February.
- **The VAT threshold** — trailing turnover approaching R1 million is a this-quarter conversation, not a someday one.
- **Entity choice** — the sole-prop-versus-company arithmetic shifts with each Budget's rates; November's framework article plus this year's numbers is a 20-minute re-run.
- **Retirement trajectory** — a new s11F ceiling opened on 1 March; freelancers who escalate the debit order every March quietly compound both the deduction and the retirement outcome.

## The Payoff Statement

Do this fortnight properly and the 2027/28 tax year consists of: twelve quiet monthly closes, one August afternoon building an estimate from live numbers, one October retirement session, and one February evening executing a payment you predicted months earlier.

App FAQ

Using the InspiredTax app

Availability, data storage, security, and pricing — answered.

When will the app be available?

InspiredTax Africa is launching soon on Google Play. Join the waitlist on the home page and we will email you once at launch.

How do I get early access?

Early access details will be shared with waitlist subscribers first.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. The app is offline-first — your figures never touch a cloud server.

How is my data protected?

All stored data is encrypted with AES-256, aligned with POPIA principles.

What does the app cost?

Pricing will be announced at launch. Early waitlist subscribers get launch pricing.

Will it update for new tax years?

Yes — SARS tables and thresholds are updated for each tax year.

What if I need help?

Email support@inspiredtax.co.za and we will get back to you.
